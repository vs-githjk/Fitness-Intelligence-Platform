# Coach-Programming Market Research — August 2026

Purpose: sharpen the Vytal Coach Programming Studio against the live market. Focus is
**exercise discovery, workout building, and program authoring** — the surfaces Vytal is
transforming — plus the trainee-execution UX that sets consumer-app expectations.

## Methodology & verification basis (read first)

- Research targeted current official product sites, help/doc centers, app-store pages, and
  reputable reviews. This doc separates **verified/stable documented functionality** from
  **inference/sentiment**, using these labels on claims:
  - **[V]** — functionality that is documented and stable across the product's lifetime
    (high confidence; consistent with primary sources). Where a specific page confirmed it,
    the URL is given.
  - **[I]** — inference, reasoning, or qualitative signal from reviews/forums (directional
    only, never treated as fact about functionality).
- **Honesty note:** live 2026 re-verification was time-boxed and is **partial**. Feature
  claims below reflect each product's long-documented, stable capability set; treat exact
  2026 pricing, brand ownership, and newly-shipped features as **[PARTIAL]** unless a URL is
  cited. Products marked **[PARTIAL]** were not exhaustively re-verified this pass. The
  strategic synthesis does not depend on any single unverified detail.
- Sources consulted (representative, accessed 2026-08-14): trainerize.com,
  truecoach.co, everfit.io, trainheroic.com, teambuildr.com, ptdistinction.com, hevyapp.com,
  hevycoach.com, strongapp.io, alphaprogression.com, joinladder.com, fitbod.me,
  musclewiki.com, darebee.com, plus G2/Capterra/App Store/Reddit for sentiment.

## Competitor findings

### Trainerize (ABC Trainerize) — all-in-one PT platform
- **Makes easier:** running an entire online-coaching business from one place — programming,
  nutrition, habits, messaging, payments, video calls. [V]
- **Table stakes present:** large exercise video library (thousands), custom exercise upload
  with video, drag-drop workout builder, supersets/circuits, "master programs" cloned and
  auto-scheduled to a client calendar, progress tracking, in-app chat. [V]
- **Discovery:** browse/search the library by name; category/muscle/equipment filtering
  exists but is list-driven, not visual. No body-map discovery. [V]/[I]
- **Program authoring:** multi-week master programs, calendar scheduling, auto-progression via
  "smart" workout duplication; edits to a master can propagate. [V]
- **Mobile programming:** coaches can build/edit from the app, though power-users prefer web. [I]
- **Bloat / complaints:** repeatedly called feature-heavy and **overwhelming**; parts of the
  UI feel dated/clunky; building large programs is tedious; occasional bugs; cost rises with
  client count. [I]
- **Visual:** functional but **dated** in places; not a design leader. [I]

### TrueCoach — coach-first, deliberately lean
- **Makes easier:** clean 1:1 online coaching; fastest path from "assign a workout" to
  "client logs it." [V]
- **Table stakes:** 1,000+ video exercise library, custom exercise upload, drag-drop builder,
  supersets, program/week duplication and templates, metrics + progress photos, messaging with
  video/voice. [V]
- **Discovery:** name/tag search over the library; no visual/body-map discovery. [V]/[I]
- **Strength:** widely praised as **simple and clean**, low learning curve — the reference for
  "not overwhelming." [I]
- **Complaints:** thinner on periodization visualization and automation than heavier tools;
  calendar/program-overview seen as limited by some strength coaches. [I]
- **Visual:** clean, modern-adjacent, restrained. [I]

### Everfit — modern UI + automation
- **Makes easier:** modern-feeling programming plus **automation** (triggers, tasks, forms,
  auto-messages) and a generous free tier. [V]/[I]
- **Table stakes:** exercise library + custom exercises, drag-drop builder, supersets, program
  calendar, in-app payments, leaderboards/teams. [V]
- **Discovery:** library search/filter; no body-map. [I]
- **Strength:** often cited as the **best-looking** of the coach tools; automation appeals to
  scaled coaches. [I]
- **Complaints:** automation adds a learning curve; some depth-vs-simplicity tension. [I]

### TrainHeroic — strength/team + marketplace [PARTIAL]
- **Makes easier:** percentage/RPE/tempo strength programming and a **marketplace** to sell
  programs; team training. [V]
- **Authoring:** block/wave programming, %1RM off tested maxes, RPE, tempo. [V]
- **Reject signal:** the **marketplace** injects browse/monetization noise that a coach-first,
  no-marketplace product (Vytal) should avoid. [I]
- **Complaints:** UI can feel clunky; marketplace clutter; price. [I]

### TeamBuildr — S&C for teams/schools, spreadsheet-speed [PARTIAL]
- **Makes easier:** **very fast bulk entry** via a spreadsheet-style calendar; auto-calculated
  loads from athlete maxes; "weight room view" for TV displays; leaderboards. [V]/[I]
- **Why it matters to Vytal:** it is the clearest proof that **serious strength coaches want
  spreadsheet-speed data entry**, not click-heavy card builders. This is the exact tension
  Vytal's positioning targets. [I]
- **Complaints:** dated UI; team-oriented (not 1:1); learning curve. [I]

### PT Distinction — highly customizable online coaching [PARTIAL]
- **Makes easier:** deep customization, assessments/forms, automation, drag-drop builder. [V]
- **Complaints:** **powerful but overwhelming**; dated UI; steep learning curve — the archetype
  of "capability without usability." [I]

### Hevy (+ Hevy Coach) — consumer logging benchmark
- **Makes easier:** the **fastest, most tactile set-logging** in the consumer market; huge
  free adoption. [V]/[I]
- **Execution UX (the bar Vytal's runner must clear):** one-tap set completion, rest timer
  auto-starts, **previous-performance shown inline per set**, plate calculator, RPE, supersets,
  routine folders, large exercise DB with custom exercises. [V]
- **Hevy Coach:** coach-side routine builder + assignment + chat; **simpler and cheaper** than
  dedicated coach tools, but shallower on periodization/automation. [V]/[I]
- **Visual:** clean, modern, mobile-first — a design reference. [I]

### Strong — minimalist logger [PARTIAL]
- **Makes easier:** frictionless logging with near-zero chrome; plate calc, rest timer,
  warm-up calculator, supersets. [V]
- **Signal:** beloved for **restraint**; slower feature development leaves room above it. No
  coach product. [I]

### Alpha Progression — hypertrophy analytics + auto-progression [PARTIAL]
- **Makes easier:** **automatic progression suggestions** and per-muscle **volume analytics**;
  exercise DB with muscle diagrams and a plan generator. [V]/[I]
- **Signal:** shows demand for deterministic progression guidance and muscle-volume feedback —
  adjacent to Vytal's deterministic ethos. [I]

### Ladder (joinladder) — premium team-based coaching [PARTIAL]
- **Makes easier:** expert-coach-led "teams"; polished, **premium consumer** presentation of a
  coach's program; video demos; community. [V]/[I]
- **Signal:** proves a market for **premium, coach-authored, beautifully-presented** training —
  Vytal's "seen, not described" ambition on the consumer side. [I]

### Fitbod — algorithmic generator (a REJECT reference)
- **Makes easier:** auto-generates a session and shows a **muscle-recovery body heatmap**;
  swap exercises; tracks per-muscle fatigue. [V]
- **Discovery:** body heatmap is a genuinely good **visual muscle-state** pattern. [V]
- **Reject signal:** persistent complaints that generated programming feels **repetitive /
  robotic / not a real coach** and over-indexes on machines — the archetype of the "fake AI
  coach" Vytal explicitly rejects. [I]

### MuscleWiki — best-in-class visual discovery
- **Makes easier:** click a muscle on an **interactive front/back body map** (male/female) →
  exercises with looping GIF demos; filter by equipment/difficulty/force. [V]
  (musclewiki.com)
- **Signal:** the **gold standard for body-map exercise discovery** and for **owned,
  consistent demonstration media** (its GIFs feel like one library). Directly validates
  Vytal's BodyMap + owned-visual direction. [V]/[I]

### DAREBEE — owned illustrated visual language
- **Makes easier:** free, coherent workout library delivered as **distinctive illustrated
  posters/PDFs**; printable; no account friction. [V] (darebee.com)
- **Signal:** proof that a **single owned illustration style** (not stock video) can make an
  exercise library feel like one premium brand — the aesthetic case for Vytal's MovementGlyph /
  owned-media direction over licensed stock video. [I]

## Synthesis — four buckets

### 1) TABLE STAKES (Vytal looks deficient without these)
- Exercise library with **search + muscle/equipment/movement filtering**. Vytal has this
  (deterministic synonym search + facets). ✅
- **Custom exercises with coach media** (image/video). Vytal has the model + a separate editor,
  but **not inline from the builder** — a real gap.
- **Workout builder** with sets/reps/load/rest, **supersets/blocks**, **reorder**,
  **duplication**, and **template/program cloning**. Vytal has builder + keyboard reorder +
  cloning; **supersets/blocks polish and drag-drop are gaps**.
- **Multi-week programs + assignment** to trainees. Vytal has starter programs + assignment. ✅
- **Editing published/assigned content with clear consequence messaging.** Vytal's immutable
  versioning covers this; the **UI must state the trainee-facing consequence in plain words.**
- **Tactile trainee execution** (rest timer, previous-performance inline, quick set entry,
  plate math). Hevy/Strong set the bar; Vytal's runner must match it.

### 2) ADOPT / IMPROVE (fits Vytal; can be done simpler/better)
- **Body-map discovery** (MuscleWiki-grade): click a region → exercises. Vytal has an interim
  geometric BodyMap; upgrade the interaction to click-to-filter.
- **Spreadsheet-speed bulk entry** (TeamBuildr): let coaches type a whole session fast, not
  just click cards. Vytal already has CSV import — extend that speed into the live builder.
- **Deterministic progression suggestions + per-muscle volume view** (Alpha Progression),
  built the Vytal way: deterministic, explainable, coach-authored — never autonomous.
- **Inline previous-performance in execution** (Hevy) — highest-leverage tactile win.
- **Owned demonstration media as one coherent library** (MuscleWiki GIFs / DAREBEE
  illustration) — Vytal's MovementGlyph + authored media, not stock video.
- **Premium program presentation** (Ladder) — the generated program/session identity Vytal
  already wants (posters, motion).

### 3) REJECT (incompatible with Vytal's principles)
- **Program marketplaces** (TrainHeroic) — browse/monetization noise; violates coach-first.
- **Algorithmic "AI coach" auto-generation** (Fitbod) — repetitive/robotic; violates
  coach-assisted-not-autonomous + no-AI-coach.
- **Feature-bloat / everything-app** creep (Trainerize, PT Distinction) — payments, CRM,
  nutrition, habits, video calls all at once; violates Premium Simplicity / Coach Ease.
- **Gamification & social-network behavior** (leaderboards-as-default, feeds) — violates Calm
  Intelligence.
- **Dashboards-for-dashboards** — metrics that don't drive a decision; violates Guidance over
  Metrics.

### 4) VYTAL OPPORTUNITIES (competitors do poorly, or fail to combine)
- **The core wedge:** MuscleWiki-grade **visual discovery** + TeamBuildr-grade **fast
  authoring** + Hevy-grade **tactile execution**, in **one** coach-first product. No competitor
  combines all three — coach tools are click-heavy and often dated; loggers are tactile but
  shallow on programming; discovery tools don't program at all.
- **Owned, coherent training visual language** end-to-end (glyphs + authored media + body map),
  where competitors rely on inconsistent stock video.
- **Deterministic, explainable, honest** intelligence as a positive differentiator against the
  "fake AI coach" fatigue Fitbod-style products created.
- **Coach Ease as product law** — non-technical coaches program excellent training without
  learning software vocabulary; PT Distinction/Trainerize prove the market punishes the
  opposite.

### Positioning test — "programming power of serious coaching software with the usability of a consumer fitness app"
**Evidence SUPPORTS it, with one caveat.** The market is genuinely bifurcated: serious
programming (TeamBuildr, TrainHeroic, PT Distinction, Trainerize) is powerful but repeatedly
called overwhelming/dated; consumer usability (Hevy, Strong) is delightful but programming-thin.
No one credibly owns the middle. The caveat: "usability of a consumer app" is set by **tactile
execution and fast entry**, not just clean screens — so the positioning is only earned once
Vytal's builder is drag-tactile *and* its runner matches Hevy. It is a real, defensible gap, not
a slogan — but it is unproven until the tactile bar is hit.

**Interpretation:** Vytal is on-strategy and already holds several table stakes (search,
cloning, real library, media model, immutable versioning). The concentrated **IMPROVE NOW**
cluster is exactly the "usability of a consumer app" half of the positioning: make the builder
**tactile and fast** (drag-drop, first-class supersets, fast entry, inline custom-exercise),
make discovery **click-a-muscle**, and make the runner **match Hevy** (inline prior set, plate
math, one-tap). Everything that would dilute the product — marketplace, AI auto-coach,
everything-app bloat, gamification — is correctly a REJECT and aligns with existing product law.
