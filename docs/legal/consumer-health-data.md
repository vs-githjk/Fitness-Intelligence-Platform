# Consumer Health Data Privacy Notice

This notice supplements our [Privacy Policy](privacy-policy.md) and addresses **consumer health
data** under laws such as the Washington *My Health My Data Act* (MHMDA) and similar state laws.
It is provided as a distinct, separately linked notice because those laws require one.

> MHMDA private-right-of-action posture before publishing. Fitness measurements and data derived
> from them may qualify as "consumer health data."

## What consumer health data we collect

Data a reasonable person could link to your health status, including: fitness assessments and
baseline measurements, readiness/status check-ins, and workout performance and history that you
record in Vytal.

## Why we collect it

Solely to provide the coaching service you signed up for: to let your coach program and adjust
your training, and to let you follow and record it. We do **not** use it for advertising and we
do **not** sell it.

## How we collect consent

- **At onboarding / invitation acceptance**, a trainee consents to collect this data and to
  share it with the **specific coach** who invited them.
- Consent is **specific** (to that coaching relationship) and **revocable**.

## Who we share it with

- **Your coach**, within your coaching relationship, and only your coach.
- **Service providers** that host and store the data on our behalf under contract.
- We do **not** sell consumer health data and do **not** share it for cross-context behavioral
  advertising. Under MHMDA, "selling" health data requires valid authorization; we do not do it.

## Your rights

You may **withdraw consent**, **access**, **delete**, or request a list of who your data has been
shared with. Withdrawing consent ends your coach's access going forward. To exercise these
rights, email `[PRIVACY_EMAIL]`. We respond within the timeframe the applicable law requires.

## Security

Consumer health data is protected by the controls on our [Security & Trust page](security.md),
and access is technically limited to you and your coach.
