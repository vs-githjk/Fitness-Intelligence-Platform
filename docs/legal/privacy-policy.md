## 1. Who this applies to

Vytal has two kinds of users:

- **Coaches**, who create exercises and training programs and invite trainees.
- **Trainees**, who are invited by a coach to receive, perform, and record training.

A coach and their trainees form a coaching relationship. Some data is deliberately **shared
within that relationship** — see Section 4.

## 2. Information we collect

We collect only what the product needs to function.

| Category | Examples | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Account information | Name, email, password (stored only as a hash), role | You |
| Coach profile | Display name, credentials text you enter | Coaches |
| Trainee profile | Timezone and profile fields you enter | Trainees |
| Fitness assessments & baseline inputs | Measurements and inputs you record to establish a baseline | Trainees |
| Readiness check-ins | Daily readiness/status inputs | Trainees |
| Workout content | Exercises, programs, workouts you author or import | Coaches |
| Workout performance & history | Sets, reps, loads, durations, completion you record | Trainees |
| Safety reports | Issues a trainee or coach flags about training | Coaches/Trainees |
| Media uploads | Images or short videos you attach to an exercise/profile | Coaches |
| Invitations | Invite records connecting a coach to a trainee | Coaches |
| Technical & security logs | Request identifiers, timestamps, IP address, coarse device/browser info | Automatically |

We do **not** collect precise geolocation, we do **not** sell personal information, and we do
**not** run third-party advertising trackers on health screens.

## 3. How we use information

- To provide the service: authenticate you, deliver coaching and training, and store your work.
- To keep the service safe and reliable: prevent abuse, debug problems, and investigate
  security incidents (using the technical/security logs).
- To communicate with you about your account and service-essential notices (e.g., invitations,
  security notifications). We do not operate a marketing-email program.

We do not use your training or health-related inputs to build advertising profiles, and we do
not use them to train machine-learning models.

## 4. How coaching relationships share data

When a trainee accepts a coach's invitation, the trainee **consents** to share training and
health-related inputs (assessments, check-ins, workout performance, safety reports) with **that
coach**, so the coach can program and adjust training. A coach can see this data only for their
own trainees. A trainee can **withdraw consent**, which ends the sharing and the coach's access
going forward. We enforce these boundaries in the software, not merely by policy.
